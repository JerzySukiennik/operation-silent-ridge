# Operation Silent Ridge

Co-op (1–4 players) carrier strike game in Godot 4.6: walk the deck of a Nimitz-class carrier, launch in an F-35C, fly a snowy canyon under enemy radar, laser-bomb an underground plant with your wingman, escape SAMs and fighters, and trap back aboard.
